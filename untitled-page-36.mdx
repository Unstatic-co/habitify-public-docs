Building great habits while juggling classes, assignments, and exams is tough, but we want to make it a little easier. That is why we created the Habitify Student Program, designed to help students achieve their goals and build lifelong routines at a fraction of the cost.

## **Student Offer Details**

Eligible students receive **50% off the Habitify Pro Annual plan** for the first year.

This program is available to students **from all over the world**. To qualify, you simply need a validated educational email address (such as a `.edu` address or your country's equivalent) that was provided directly by your school or university.

## **Claiming Your Student Discount**

### **Recommendation**

We highly recommend completing this entire process on your **mobile phone** for the smoothest setup experience, as the discount link will easily open directly in your Habitify app.

### **Step-by-step Guide**

1. Open the Habitify app and navigate to the **Upgrade** tab.
2. Scroll all the way down to the bottom of the screen, <br />find and tap **'Are you a student?'**
3. Provide your official school email address,<br />Tap the **'Send my Discount'** button.
4. Go to the inbox for your school email account and look for an email from Habitify with the subject line: _"Claim your Habitify Premium Student Discount"._<br />_(Note: Be sure to check your spam or junk folders if you don't see it within a few minutes!)_
5. Click the link provided inside the email, and follow the prompted steps to get the offer.

</Accordion>
