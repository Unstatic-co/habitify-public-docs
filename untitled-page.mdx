title: "Bad habits"
---

A Bad Habit is a behavior you'd rather do less of, or quit entirely, because it works against the life you're trying to build. It can be something small and easy to overlook, like checking your phone the moment you wake up, or something bigger, like smoking or staying up too late.

In Habitify, tracking a Bad Habit isn't about guilt, it's about awareness. Seeing the pattern clearly is often the first step to breaking it. This article covers everything you need to know to work with Bad Habits in Habitify - from adding one, to logging your progress as you cut back.

  <Tab title="iOS">
    ## Adding a bad habit

    1. In **Journal**, tap the **\+ button** at the bottom right corner.
    2. Choose '**Break Bad Habit**'.
    3. Choose a **pre-defined habit template**, or tap '**Custom Habit**' to set everything from scratch.
    4. Configure the details of the bad habit.
    5. Once everthing is set, **tap ✓ button** to save the bad habit to your tracking list.

    ## Details of a bad habit

    ### Icon & Name

    The icon and name of your bad habit helps it easily be identified across your Journal, Progress view, and everywhere else in the app.

    ### Goal

    Goal of a bad habit defines what "success" means for the habit, and it comes in two types:

    - **No More Than (limit-type)**: you set a maximum, and your goal is to stay at or under it each day. Go over, and the day is marked Failed once your logged total exceeds the limit.
    - **Quit This Habit (quit-type)** : there's no numeric limit; the goal is zero occurrences. A single instance of the habit fails the day.

    ### Start date

    The date from which your progress on this habit is saved in Habitif. It's also the date the habit begins appearing on your Journal view.

    ## How a bad habit appears in the list

    The bad habit will start showing in your Journal since its start date.

    Since there is no settings of _Repeat_, _Area_, and _Time of Day_ for the bad habits, these ones always appear in the **'All habits' list** and the **'Time of Day' list.**

    - **For the quit-type habits and the limit-type ones with goal per day**: categorized under '_Habit to Break_' section within the list.
    - **For the limit-type habits with goal per week, per month, or per year**: categorized followingly under '_This Week_', '_This Month_', '_This Year_' sections.

    ## Recording progress of a bad habit

    ### Status of a bad habit

    A bad habit can possibly carry one of three statuses: **Succeeded, Skipped** or **Failed**.

    - **Succeeded**: your total logged value for the day is 0, or within your set limit.
    - **Skipped**: you went over the limit or did the habit, but chose to treat it as a cheat day rather than break your streak. A skipped day freezes your streak instead of resetting it.
    - **Failed** : your total logged value exceeds the limit (or, for a quit-type habit, any occurrence at all).

    <Info>
      At the end of each day, if your logged value is still 0 or under the limit, the habit is automatically marked as successful. You don't need to manually confirm a good day.
    </Info>

    <Info>
      If you set the Start Date to a day in the past, every day from that date up through yesterday is automatically marked as Succeeded.
    </Info>

    ### How to check in on a bad habit

    To manually check in on a bad habit:

    - **Use smart button**: tap the **'Succeed'** button on the right of the habit in the Journal to mark it as succeeded for the day.
    - **Use swipe gestures**: wipe the habit right for **Succeed** or **Log Progress**; swipe it left for **Skip** or **Fail**.

    <Note>
      **When marking a habit as "Failed",** what gets logged behind the scenes depends on the habit's goal type:

      - **Quit-type habits**: marking Failed automatically logs "1" as the progress value for the current period, since a single occurrence is the entire event.
      - **Limit-type habits**: marking Failed flags the day as failed, but logs a progress value of zero. Habitify doesn't know how much you actually did, so it doesn't guess. If you want the real amount reflected, log it yourself using **Log Progress**.
    </Note>

    ## FAQs

    <Accordion title="How to undo a wrong log added for a bad habit?">
      If the habit already has a status for the day:

      - In _Journal_, swipe the habit right and choose '**Undo'**
      - Pick either '**Remove Today Data'** (resets today's value to 0) or '**Edit Log Manually'** (swipe left on the specific log entry and choose **Delete**).

      If the habit is still in progress:

      - In _Journal_, tap on the habit
      - In next screen, tap the (•••) button in the top right corner, and choose '**Log History**'
      - In the _Log History_, swipe left the log you want to remove and tap '**Delete**'
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How to change the status of a bad habit on a date in past?">
      To log progress for a past date, tap on the bad habit, then tap the (•••) button in the top right corner, choose **Log Progress**, then select the date and enter your value.
    </Accordion>

    You can assign a Bad Habit to one or more Areas, like "Health" or "Personal Growth", so it shows up alongside your Good Habits when you're reviewing that part of your life, instead of being visible only in a separate Bad Habits view. 

    A single habit isn't limited to one Area; assign it to as many as make sense.

    ## How a bad habit appears in the list

    Since there is no _'Repeat'_ and _'Time of Day'_ settings for bad habits, the bad habits will appear in the **'All Habits'**, **'Time of Day' lists**, and **the list of its areas** everyday.

    Where a Bad Habit lands in within each list in the view depends on its goal type:

    - **Limit-type habits with a daily goal** sit together with your Good Habits that also have a daily goal; both are core, everyday actions expected of you today, so they're grouped as one.
    - **Quit-type habits** get their own separate sub-category, '**Quit Habit(s)'**, since there's no daily numeric goal to hit, just a day to get through without the behavior happening at all.
    - **Limit-type habits with a weekly, monthly, or yearly goal** are categorized followingly to the section **'This Week'**, '**This Month'**, **'This Year'** together with the good habits.

    Like any habit, **once it's checked** in for the day, it moves into the **'Completed'**, **'Skipped'**, or **'Failed'** groups depending on the outcome, keeping what's still open clearly separate from what's already resolved.

    ### How to check in on a bad habit

    In Journal, how you check in depends on the habit's goal type:

    **For quit-type habits:**

    - Tap the ••• **button** on the right of the bad habit and choose between 
      - **Mark as Done**: confirm you successfully avoided it for the day (Succeeded).
      - **Reset my Streak:** log a slip and start over (Failed).
    - **Swipe the habit left** for the **Skip** option.

    **For limit-type habits:**

    - Tap the smart button to add a progress log, the smart button shows as:
      - **'\+1'** for habits with the limit as 1 time
      - **'\+1'** for habits with any limit set.
    - Swipe the habit **left** for **Skip** or **Fail**.
    - Swipe the habit **right** for **Succeed** or **Log Value**.
