This is a short guide of how to add a good habit and track it in Habitify app.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title=" iOS">
    ## Adding a good habit

    <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Sm2g8KAFHdm67Je7YV6KH6qSpUps2tDN/preview" width="640" height="360" />

    1. In Habitify iOS  app, access Journal tab.
    2. Tap on + button at the bottom right corner, then choose **Build Good Habit**.
    3. Choose a pre-defined habit template, <br />or tap **Custom Habit** to build a new one from scratch.
    4. Configure **Repeat**, **Goal**, and **Reminder** for your new habit.
    5. When everything is set, tap the ✓ button in the top right corner to save the new habit to your list and start tracking

    ## Tracking progress of a good habit

    From the **Day** view of **Journal**, these are the ways to record your progress on a habit.

    - Swipe right, and choose **Complete** to mark your habit as Done, or **Log** to record a value to your habit progress.
    - Swipe left, and choose **Skip** to mark this day as a break, or **Fail** to mark it as a slip.

    Alternatively, in **Week** view, you can:

    - Tap on a cell or progress stripe, to add +1 or log a value to your habit progress.
    - Hold on a day, and choose to mark it with **Skip** or **Fail**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="🤖 Android">
    ## Adding a new good habit

    <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eq5cs-OzScF595HEu2RXYL_z81mkbO5h/preview" width="640" height="360" />

    1. In the Habitify Android app, access the Journal tab
    2. Tap the + button near the bottom right corner, then choose **Create Good Habit**
    3. Choose a pre-defined habit template,<br />or tap **Create Your Own** to build from scratch.
    4. Configure **Repeat**, set **Goal**, and add **Reminders** for your new habit.<br />For each change, remember to tap ← to save change and go back.
    5. Once everything is set, tap the Save button to confirm the new habit to your list.

    ## Tracking progress of a good habit

    From the **Day** view of **Journal**, these are the ways to record your progress on a habit.

    - Swipe right, and choose **Complete** to mark your habit as Done, or **Log value** to record a value to your habit progress.
    - Swipe left, and choose **Skip** to mark this day as a break, or **Fail** to mark it as a slip.

    Alternatively, in **Week** view, you can:

    - Tap on a cell or progress stripe, to add +1 or log a value to your habit progress.
    - Hold on a day, and choose to mark it with **Skip** or **Fail**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="🖥️ Website & Desktop">
    ## Adding a good habit

    <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W4NzQD2KMWiJ-o1xODD0iIZVpXDP5Cok/preview" width="640" height="360" />

    1. From the left navigation bar, access the **All Habits** tab.
    2. Navigate to the bottom action bar, click **\+ Add Habit**, then choose **Create Good Habit**.
    3. Click the name field and choose one from the pre-defined habit template.<br />Or simply enter the name of your own habit, and start with a scratch.
    4. Schedule your habit in **Repeat**, set **Goal**, and add **Reminders** for your new habit.
    5. Once everything is set, click the **Save** button at the bottom right corner to save the new habit to your tracking list

    ## Tracking progress of a good habit

    In **Grid** layout, right-click on any cell, and choose an action to mark your habit as Completed, Skipped, or Failed. You can choose multiple cells to check-in at once.

    In **List** layout, right-click a habit, or hover on the habit and click the 3-dot menu button near the end, and choose an action to mark your habit with Completed, Skipped, or Failed.
