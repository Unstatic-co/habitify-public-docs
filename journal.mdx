The Journal is where your habits actually live day to day. It's the first screen you see when you open Habitify, showing exactly what's due today and nothing more. No matter which platform you're on, the Journal is built around the same idea: keep today simple and clear, while still giving you the tools to organize, filter, and sort your habits underneath that simplicity.

This article walks through how to navigate the Journal, understand how your habits are grouped, and manage the order they appear in, so you can shape it to fit how you actually move through your day.

    ## Switching between lists

    At the top of the Journal, right next to **All Habits**, you'll find an Area Bar that lets you move between different ways of viewing your day:

    ![Areabar Journal I OS](/images/Areabar_Journal_iOS.png "Areabar Journal I OS")

    - **All Habits**: every habit scheduled for today, in one list.
    - **Current Time of Day list:** only the habits assigned to that block of time. This automatically jumps to the current time block based on the ranges you've set (for example, switching to "Morning" the moment your morning window starts), so you're not stuck manually flipping tabs throughout the day.
    - **Custom Area lists**: habits grouped under the Areas you've created, like "Health" or "Work." Instead of scanning your whole day, you can zoom into just one part of your life at a time.

    ## Sub-categories within a list

    Within any list, Habitify quietly groups habits by their current status, so what needs your attention rises to the top: 

    ![Habit Status Journal I OS Copy](/images/HabitStatus_Journal_iOS-copy.png "Habit Status Journal I OS Copy")

    - **In progress / uncompleted**: habits you haven't finished yet today. This is the default focus of your list.
    - **Completed**: habits you've already checked off, kept visible so you can see today's wins at a glance.
    - **Skipped**: habits that you chose to log it as a cheat day rather than break your streak. A skipped day freezes your streak instead of resetting it.
    - **Failed**: habits you're marking honestly as a slip, and want it recorded as-is rather than smoothed over as a skip.

    Within the in-progress/uncompleted group specifically, habits are further ordered by their goal type, top to bottom: 

    ![Habit Type Journal I OS](/images/HabitType_Journal_iOS.png "Habit Type Journal I OS")

    - **Habits with a goal per day**: sits at the top, together, since both are core, everyday actions you're expected to act on today.
    - **Quit Habit(s)**: habits to quit, or habits to limit with a daily goal.
    - **This Week**: good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per week.
    - **This Month**: good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per month.
    - **This Year**: good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per year.

    ## Managing habit order

    <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cKK8d2w8I12G02TvZX4_9q0icU_Kni-L/preview" width="640" height="360" />

    Tap the sort button in the top-right corner of the Journal to arrange your list by:

    - **A-Z or Z-A: habits are ordered** alphabetically by their name.
    - **Reminder Time:** habits ordered chronologically their first reminder in the day.
    - **My Habits Order: habits are** positioned however you decide, manually.

    <Note>
      These settings are only applied for the habit order in the 'All Habits' list
    </Note>

    ### How to customize 'My Habits Order'

    1. In _Journal,_ view thew '**All Habits**' list.
    2. Tap on **the sort button** on the date bar.
    3. Tap **the sort button** again, then choose '**Reorder Habits**'.
    4. Hold on **the ☰ icon** on the right of the habit, then drag it up or down to its new position.
    5. When everything is set, tap **the ✓ button** on the top right corner to save the changes.

    <Note>
      **Order is independent per list.** 

      Rearranging “All Habits” doesn’t change the order inside a Time of Day list or a Custom Area lists. 

      - To manage the habit order in each Time of Day list: go to Settings → Time of Day → choose time block → arrange the habits in ‘Included’ section.
      - To manage the habit order in each Custom Area list: go to Settings → All Areas → choose an area → arrange the habits in ‘Included’ list section.
    </Note>

    ## **Searching habits**

    <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cCGRzUANz0gI0XlhaJiXkoFPezvG1hud/preview" width="640" height="360" />

    To navigate through your long habit list and find the habit you want:

    - In _Journal_, hold and drag down your habit list.
    - Hold your screen until you see **the 🔍︎ button**, then drag it to the right to select the function.
    - Enter the habit you want to access in the search, the result shows immediately based on your input.
    - From the search result, you can check in on your habit directly.

    To exit the search, simply tap on ‘**Cancel**’.

    ## Switching between lists

    At the top of the Journal, right next to **All Habits**, you'll find an Area Bar that lets you move between different ways of viewing your day:

    - **All Habits**: every habit scheduled for today, in one list.
    - **Time of Day lists:** only the habits assigned to that block of time. This bar is smart: it automatically jumps to the current time block based on the ranges you've set (for example, switching to "Morning" the moment your morning window starts), so you're not stuck manually flipping tabs throughout the day.

    Each of these is a genuinely separate list: a habit's position in "All Habits" doesn't affect where it sits in a Time of Day list or a Custom Area, which is what makes the next section useful.

    ## Switching between layouts

    Journal has two layouts: **Day View** and **Week View**. _Daily View_ keeps your attention on today. _Weekly View_ shows a full week at a glance, so you can spot patterns.

    To switch between the layouts, tap the layout button next to "All Habits".

    **Day View** and **Week View** show the same habits from the same list. Only the way each habit is displayed changes.

    ### Day View

    Day View shows exactly what's due today and nothing more. It's the place to check in, log progress, and see what's left of your day.

    ![Day View Journal Android](/images/DayView_Journal_Android.png "Day View Journal Android")

    Within any list, Habitify quietly groups habits by their current status, so what needs your attention rises to the top:

    - **In progress / uncompleted**: habits you haven't finished yet today. This is the default focus of your list.
    - **Completed**: habits you've already checked off, kept visible so you can see today's wins at a glance.
    - **Skipped**: habits that you chose to log it as a cheat day rather than break your streak. A skipped day freezes your streak instead of resetting it.
    - **Failed**: habits you're marking honestly as a slip, and want it recorded as-is rather than smoothed over as a skip.

    Within the in-progress/uncompleted group specifically, habits are further ordered by their goal type, top to bottom:

    - **Good habits and limit-type habits with a daily goal**: sits at the top, together, since both are core, everyday actions you're expected to act on today.
    - **Quit Habit(s)**: habits you're trying to quit entirely, kept in their own sub-category since there's no daily goal to hit, just a day to get through.
    - **This Week**: Good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per week.
    - **This Month**: Good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per month.
    - **This Year**: Good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per year.

    ### Week View

    <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YamN5PUfQ3494cWsGe2bco11ZL2EGHZH/preview" width="640" height="360" />

    Week View shows your habits across a full week, so you can see how they're trending instead of only what's due today. 

    The week starts on Monday or Sunday, depending on your selection in **Settings > First Day of Week**.

    Every habit gets its own row, with its icon and name above it. Today is highlighted in the day bar at the top. To move between weeks, use the week bar at the bottom of the screen. The selected week is highlighted.

    How a habit looks depends on its type: **a good habit**, **a habit to limit**, or **a habit to quit**.

    ![Weekly View Good Habits Journal](/images/WeeklyView-GoodHabits-Journal.png "Weekly View Good Habits Journal")

    If you habit is **a good habit with goal per day**

    - Each day is one cell representing your progress of the day:
      - **A completed day** is filled with a solid shade of the habit's color. Consecutive completed days join into one continuous bar.
      - **A day still in progress** fills from the bottom up as you log more toward the daily goal.
      - **A skipped day** is marked with an arrow.
      - **A failed day** is marked with an ✕.
    - On days when the habit doesn't repeat or haven't started yet, the cell is hatched with diagonal lines.
    - Your streak appears as a 🔥 with its length (for example, 🔥**7d**) on the cell of the last day you completed the daily goal. If the whole week is part of a streak, it appears on the last day of the week and shows your streak length up to that day.

    If you habit is **a good habit with goal per week, month, or year**

    - The period is one seamless stripe 
    - The stripe shows your progress toward the goal and fills as you log. For example, **2/7 times** for a weekly goal, or **27/30 km this month** for a monthly goal.
    - On the right of the habit name is your streak, counted in weeks, months, or years to match the goal period (for example, 🔥**2w** or 🔥**3m**). It's counted up to the week you're viewing, so it may changes as you move between weeks.

    ![Weekly View Bad Habits Android](/images/WeeklyView-BadHabits-Android.png "Weekly View Bad Habits Android")

    If you habit is **a bad habit to quit**

    Habits to quit have no goal period. They're tracked day by day.

    - Each day is one cell representing your day:
      - **A day you stayed on track** is filled with a solid shade of the habit's color. Consecutive days join into one continuous bar.
      - **A day you check in 'Fail'** is marked with an ✕.
      - **A day you check in 'Skip'** is marked with an →.
    - On days before your habit's start date, the cell is hatched with diagonal lines.
    - Your streak appears as a 🔥 with its length (for example, 🔥**3d**) on the cell of the last day you stayed on track.

    If you habit is **a bad habit to limit per day**

    - Each day is one cell that shows the amount you logged that day (for example, **15m**), with a marker under the amount that shows where you stand: 
      - a grey •󠁏 means you're within your limit.
      - an orange ▲ means you've reached your limit.
      - a red ✕ means you've overdone it.
    - a day you check in 'Skip' directly is marked with an →.
    - a day you check in 'Fail' directly is marked with an ✕.

    If you habit is **a bad habit to limit per week, month, or year**

    - The period is one seamless stripe with a **\+** button on its left, the stripe is extended until 'tomorrow' in the current week, and will lengthen gradually until the end of the week.
    - The stripe shows how much you've logged against your limit for that period. For monthly and yearly limits, it also shows how much you added during the week you're viewing.
    - The stripe shows your progress against the limit and fills as you log. For example, **27/60 minutes** for a weekly goal, or **2/5 times this month** for a monthly goal.

    ## Managing habit order

    <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/18XRiIWdDYgHPnpRJGdDcZI7jqPOa8tYC/preview" width="640" height="360" />

    The sorting button sits on the left side of the date bar, at the bottom of the screen, just above the navigation bar. Tap it to arrange your list by:

    - **A-Z or Z-A**: sort your habits alphabetically by their name.
    - **Reminder Time:** sort your habits ordered chronologically by their first reminder of the day.
    - **My Habits Order:** sort your habits manually, positioned however you decide.

    <Note>
      This is for sorting habits in 'All Habits' list only.
    </Note>

    ### How sorting is applied in each layout

    - **Day View**: habits are sub-categorized first (by status, and by goal type within In progress / uncompleted), then sorted in your selected order inside each group.
    - **Week View**: habits are sorted in your selected order across one continuous list, regardless of their status, type, or goal period.

    ### How to customize 'My Habits Order'

    - In Journal, access **All Habits** list
    - Navigate to the date bar, tap the sort button.
    - In _Sort by_, choose **My Habits Order**.
    - Access the menu again, in _Manage_, choose **My Habits Order**.
    - Press and hold the ⚌ icon, then drag it up or down to its new position.
    - Tap **Save** to apply your changes.

    <Note>
      **Order is independent per list.**

      Rearranging 'All Habits' doesn't change the order inside a Time of Day list or a Custom Area. To rearrange the habit order in those lists:

      - **For each Time of Day list**: go to Settings → Time of Day → choose a time block → arrange the habits.
      - **For each Custom Area list**: go to Settings → Manage Area → choose an area → arrange the habits.
    </Note>

    ## Navigation sidebar

    The sidebar on the left side of the app window is your starting point for every session. From here, you can move between:

    - **All Habits**: every habit you're tracking, scheduled for today, shown together in one place.
    - **Time of Day lists:** narrows the list down to only the habits assigned to that block of your day, so you're not looking at your evening routine while you're still having breakfast.
    - **Area lists**: if you've grouped habits under Areas like "Health," "Work," or "Morning Routine," each one gets its own list here, letting you focus on a single part of your life at a time instead of your whole day at once.

    Whichever list you pick from the sidebar, it stays selected as you switch between Grid and List layout above it, the sidebar decides _which_ habits you're looking at, and the layout switch decides _how_ you're looking at them.

    ## Main view of Habit List

    ### Grid Layout

    Grid view is built for seeing your habits the way a calendar laid out across several days at once, so you can spot patterns instead of just checking off one day in isolation.

    **How daily habits appear**

    Each daily habit gets its own row, and each day is represented as a single cell in that row, shaded in the habit's assigned color.

    Scan across a row and you're essentially looking at a visual streak, solid color chains together into an obvious run of consistency, while gaps or lighter cells show you exactly where things slipped.

    **How weekly, monthly, and yearly habits appear**

    These target-based habits render as a single horizontal stride that spans the entire length of that week, month, or year.

    Think of it as a progress bar built into the grid: it fills in gradually as you log progress, so at a glance you can tell how close you are to hitting the goal for that period without doing any mental math about which days you've already covered.

    **How habits are categorized**

    The Grid layout doesn't split your habits into completed, in-progress, or skipped groups. It's one continuous, seamless list. The idea is that Grid layout is for reviewing patterns across time, not managing today's to-do list, so breaking it up by status would work against the point of scanning everything at once.

    ### List Layout

    List layout is the narrower, more immediate counterpart: instead of a wide view across days, it zeroes in on a single day and what still needs to get done. It opens by default to **Today's** list.

    In List Layout, habits are automatically sorted into sub-categories based on their current status:

    - **In progress / uncompleted**: the habits you haven't finished yet, and the default focus of the list.
    - **Completed**: habits you've already checked off, kept visible so you can see your wins for the day so far.
    - **Skipped**: habits where you went over a limit or did something you're trying to quit, but chose to log it as a cheat day rather than break your streak. A skipped day freezes your streak instead of resetting it.
    - **Failed**: habits you're marking honestly as a slip, and want it recorded as-is rather than smoothed over as a skip.

    Within the in-progress/uncompleted group, there's a further layer of ordering based on each habit's goal type, top to bottom:

    - **Good Habits with a daily goal**: sits at the very top, since these are the core actions expected of you today.
    - **Habits to Break**: habits you're quitting entirely, or limit-type habits with a daily goal.
    - **This Week**: good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per week.
    - **This Month**: good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per month.
    - **This Year**: good habits and limit-type habits with a goal per year.

    ## Sorting habits

    Sorting behaves identically whether you're in Grid or List view. Click the sort button at the top-right corner of the habit name column, and choose from:

    - **Alphabetical (A-Z or Z-A)**: orders your list by habit name.
    - **Reminder Time**: orders habits chronologically by their first reminder of the day.
    - **My Habits Order**: a fully manual order you set yourself

    #### How to customize 'My Habit Order'

    You can set your custom order from either of two places, and both update the same underlying order, so it doesn't matter which one you use, the result carries over to both Grid and List layout:

    **With Grid layout:**

    1. select **My Habits Order** from the sort menu,
    2. hold your mouse on the (☷) button on the left side of the habit's icon, and drag it up or down to its new position.

    **With List layout:**

    1. on the left side bar, navigate to **References**, click on **Habit.**
    2. click on **Active(##)**
    3. hold your mouse on the habit, and drag it up or down to its new position.

    ## Searching habits

    Above the habit list, right next to the Grid/List switch, you'll find a 🔍︎ icon. Click it to pull up a quick search field and find a specific habit by name.

    This can become handy once your list has grown past what you can scan in a glance, and it works exactly the same way regardless of which layout you're currently viewing.
