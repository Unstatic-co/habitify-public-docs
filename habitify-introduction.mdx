Habitify is more than just a habit tracker; it's your personal companion for building a better, more disciplined life, one habit at a time. Our app is designed to be simple, powerful, and available everywhere you need it.

Our approach to habit building is centered on three core concepts:

1. **Track What Matters:** Set flexible goals for your habits—whether you want to do them **daily, weekly, or monthly**. Habitify adapts to your lifestyle.
2. **Stay Focused:** Your main screen is a clean, daily list that shows only the habits scheduled for **today**. This helps you focus on what's important right now, eliminating distractions.
3. **Build Momentum:** We encourage you to build the longest progress streak possible. Seeing your success chain grow is one of the most powerful motivators to keep you going.

# **Multi-Platform Supported**

We believe that tracking your habits should be convenient. That's why Habitify is available on a wide range of platforms, with your data seamlessly synced across all of them.

# **Build Good Habits, Break Bad Ones**

Habitify helps you on both fronts of your personal growth journey:

- **Create Good Habits:** Set positive goals you want to build up.
- **Track Bad Habits:** Monitor habits you want to break down and quit for good.

# **Wide Ranges of Tools**

Beyond a simple checklist, Habitify provides powerful tools to help you understand and master your habits:

- **Notes:** Write a journal entry for each habit to understand the "why" behind your progress.
- **Timer & Stopwatch:** Stay focused during duration-based habits like meditation or studying.
- **Checklists:** Break down complex habits (like a "Morning Routine") into smaller, more manageable steps.
- **Widgets:** Keep your habits top-of-mind with at-a-glance progress right on your phone's home screen.
