---
title: "Habi AI"
---

Habi is your personal assistant on the journey of building and maintaining consistency with the habits you're tracking in Habitify. 

Instead of manually navigating menus to check in, adjust a goal, or dig through your history, you can just ask. Habi understands your habit data and can act on it directly, in natural language.

## Where to find Habi AI

From **Journal,** hold and drag down the habit list until **"Ask Habi"** appears at the top of your screen, then drag down once more to select it.

From **Progress** tab or the **single progress view** of each habit, tap the AI chat icon in the bottom-right corner.

## What Habi can learn for context

- **All of your habit data**, including but not limited to:
  - Habits
  - Habit notes
  - Habit logs
  - Time of Day
  - Areas
  - Preferences / Settings
  **With your permission**, Habi can also draw on:
  - **Apple Calendar, Outlook Calendar, and Google Calendar**: your calendars and events.
  - **Apple Reminders** : your tasks and task lists.
  - **Apple Health** (iOS) or **Health Connect** (Android) all supported health data types; you choose which ones to add as context.

## What Habi can do

- **Reads what's on your screen**, so it understands what you're already looking at:
  - **Journal**: the filter you're currently using, the date you're on, and which habits are currently on screen.
  - **Single progress**: which habit you're currently viewing.
  - **Progress**:  your current filter and date range.
- **Manages your logs:**
  - Add, delete, or update a log for a habit on a specific day - Skip, Fail, or Success.
  - Add, delete, or update notes on a habit.
  - Add, delete, or update mood logs.
- **Manages your habits:**
  - Create a new habit, Good or Bad.
  - Update a habit, including adding or removing reminders, adding or removing checklist items, updating goals, updating icons.
  - Archiving or unarchiving habits.
  - Find icons for a habit.
- **Manages your areas:**
  - Create or update an area.
  - Add habits into an area, or remove habits from one.
- **Manages your Time of Day:**
  - Create or update a Time of Day.
  - Add habits into a Time of Day, or remove habits from one.
- **Answers your questions:**
  - Reads Habitify's documentation directly from the help center, so it can answer how-to questions about the app itself, not just act on your data.
