---
title: "ChatGPT Connector"
---

You can connect your Habitify account to ChatGPT using our MCP server. Once set up, you can manage your habits directly through ChatGPT conversations.

## **Prerequisites**

- A ChatGPT Plus, Team, or Enterprise subscription
- A Habitify account

## **Setup Instructions**

### **1. Open ChatGPT Settings**

1. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com/)
2. Click your profile icon in the bottom-left corner
3. Select **Settings**

### **2. Add the MCP Server**

1. Navigate to the **Tools** or **Connected Apps** section
2. Click **Add Integration** or **Add MCP Server**
3. Enter the following details:

| **Field** | **Value** |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Name** | Habitify |
| **Server URL** | `https://mcp.habitify.me/mcp` |

4. Click **Save** or **Connect**
5. You'll be redirected to sign in to your Habitify account and authorize access

### **3. Verify the Connection**

Start a new conversation and try asking:

> "Show me all my habits for today"

If everything is configured correctly, ChatGPT will fetch your habits from Habitify and display them.

## **Example Prompts**

Once connected, you can try:

- "What habits do I have scheduled for today?"
- "Mark my 'Morning Run' habit as completed"
- "Log 30 minutes for my 'Reading' habit"
- "What's my current streak for meditation?"
- "Show me my habit statistics for this week"

## **Troubleshooting**

### **Connection fails**

- Make sure you completed the sign-in and authorization step
- Check that the server URL is exactly `https://mcp.habitify.me/mcp`

### **ChatGPT doesn't use Habitify tools**

- Try explicitly mentioning "Habitify" or "my habits" in your prompt
- Start a new conversation if the tools don't appear
- Check that the integration is still enabled in your settings
