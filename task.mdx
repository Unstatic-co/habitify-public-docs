title: "Task"
---

A Task is a small, one-time to-do you can attach to a specific habit, separate from the habit's main goal, but still tied to it. Think of it as a way to capture the little supporting actions that come up around a habit without cluttering the habit itself: a book to pick up before you start your "Read" habit, or gear to buy before your first gym session.

Unlike the habit it belongs to, a Task doesn't repeat. It's created once, completed once, and then it's done.

## Task vs. Checklist

Both live inside a habit, but they solve different problems:

- **Task**: a one-time to-do. Create it, complete it once, done.
- **Checklis:** a repeatable set of steps that resets and applies every time the habit comes up (e.g., a fixed morning routine checklist inside your "Morning Routine" habit).

If what you need happens once, use a Task. If it repeats alongside the habit itself, use a Checklist.

    ## Adding a task

    1. In _Journal_, tap on your habit.
    2. In the _single progress view_, tap the **•••**  in the top right corner.
    3. Choose  **About this Habit**.
    4. Under **Tasks**, tap **New Task**.
    5. Enter the tap name, and set date & time for the task.
    6. Finally, tap **✓** or **↵** on your keyboard to save the new task.

    ## Completing a task

    1. In **Journal**, tap on your habit.
    2. In the single progress view, tap the **•••** in the top right corner.
    3. Choose **About this Habit**.
    4. Under **Tasks**, tap the 〇 before the task to mark it as completed.

    As soon as it's marked complete, it's removed from that list the next time you visit the view, keeping your habit's page focused on what's still outstanding.

    ## Deleting a task

    To delete an pending task:

    1. In **Journal**, tap on your habit.
    2. In the single progress view, tap **•••**  in the top right corner.
    3. Choose **About this Habit**.
    4. Under **Tasks**_,_ swipe the task to the left and choose **Delete**.

    1. In **Journal**, tap on your habit to open its single progress view.
    2. In the single progress view, switch to **About** tab.
    3. Under **Tasks**, tap the **〇** next to your task to complete it.

    Once you've handled a task, mark it as complete directly from the habit's **About** tab.

    ## Deleting a task

    1. In **Journal**, tap on your habit to open its single progress view
    2. In the single progress view, switch to **About** tab.
    3. Under **Tasks**, swipe the task to the left and choose **Delete**.

    ## Reviewing all tasks

    Marking a task complete doesn't erase the record of it. You can always look back at what you've finished:

    1. In the single progress view of your habit, tap the **(⋮) button** in the top-right corner.
    2. Tap to open **Manage Action List**.

    This gives you a running record of the actions that you've taken or is still pending to support the habit, alongside your regular progress.

    ## Adding a task

    1. From the view of your habit list, open the single progress view of your habit:
       1. in **Grid**: single-click on the habit.
       2. in **List**: double-click on the habit
    2. In the single progress view, click **the sidebar toggle button** in the top right corner.
    3. On the right sidebar, click **About.**
    4. Under **Action List**, click **Add new action** and enter your new task, then press Enter on your keyboard to save.
    5. Click to **Tomorrow 09:00** under the task to adjust the deadline to do the task.

    ## Completing a task

    1. From the view of your habit list. open the single progress view of a habit:
       1. in **Grid**: single-click on the habit.
       2. in **List**: double-click on the habit.
    2. In the single progress view, click the **sidebar toggle button** in the top right corner.
    3. On the right sidebar, click **About**.
    4. Under **Action List**, click 〇 before your task to mark it as done.

    ## Deleting a task

    1. From the view of your habit list. open the single progress view of a habit:
       1. in **Grid**: single-click on the habit.
       2. in **List**: double-click on the habit.
    2. In the single progress view, click **the sidebar toggle button** in the top right corner.
    3. On the right sidebar, click **About**.
    4. Under **Action List**, click ••• on the right of the task, then choose **Delete Action**.
