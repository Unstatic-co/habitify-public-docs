---
title: "Status Update"
---

Some days get away from you,  and a habit you genuinely didn't do can just sit there with no status at all, unless you remember to open the app and mark it yourself. 

**The Automation of Status Update** helps you close that gap: at a time you choose, Habitify checks for habits with no logged progress and automatically marks them for you, so your tracking stays honest even on the days you forget to check in.

## Important notes

A few limits are worth understanding so the automation behaves the way you expect:

- **It only runs on the current day.** Avoid setting the trigger time later than 11:59 PM, running past midnight risks it checking the next day's habits instead.
- **It only applies to Good Habits with a daily repeat setting.** Weekly habits, monthly habits, and bad habits aren't covered by this automation yet.
- **It only affects habits where today's progress is still 0.** Anything you've already logged something for is left alone.
- **If your device's timezone changes, manually or automatically from travel**, go back into the Status Update setting and refresh it (turn it off, then back on) so the trigger time stays accurate.
- **It's an assist, not a replacement.** You can still manually update any habit's status whenever you want, this automation just catches what you didn't get around to.

## Enabling the automation

<Tabs>
  <Tab title=" iOS">
    ![Automation I OS](/images/Automation-iOS.png "Automation I OS")

    1. In Habitify app, go to the **Settings**.
    2. Under **Integration**, open the **Automation** section.
    3. In Automation's settings screen, switch the toggle button to **ON**.
    4. In **Scheduled Time**, tap on the time and adjust to the one you prefer.
    5. In **Mark Unlogged Habits As**, choose what unlogged habits should become:
       - **Skipped**: you intentionally chose not to do it for a valid reason.
       - **Failed**: you didn't complete it, without a valid reason.
    6. Once everything is one set, tapく in the top right corner to save all and go back.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="🤖 Android">
    ![Status Update Android](/images/StatusUpdate-Android.png "Status Update Android")

    1. In Habitify app, go to the **Settings**.
    2. Under **Automation**, open the **Status Update**.
    3. In the Status Update's settings screen:
       - Tap **Everyday on [time]**, and adjust to the time when the automation will run everyday.
    4. Under **Mark Habit As**, choose what unlogged habits should become:
       - **Skipped**: you intentionally chose not to do it for a valid reason.
       - **Failed**: you didn't complete it, without a valid reason.
    5. Under Excluded Habits, tap **Add Excluded Habits...**, then select habits that is excluded from the automation.
    6. Under Status, for **Enabled**, switch the toggle ON.
    7. Once everything is set, tap **←** in the top right corner to save and return.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
