title: "Apple Calendar"
---

Your habits don't exist separately from the rest of your day. They sit right alongside your meetings, appointments, and everything else on your calendar. 

This integration syncs your Habitify habits directly into Apple Calendar, so you can see and manage them in the same place you already manage your schedule, instead of switching between two separate apps to get the full picture of your day.

## How to set it up

1. In _Habitify iOS app_, go to **Settings**.
2. Navigate to _Integration_, and tap to open **Apple Calendar**.
3. For the first time setting it up, you may be asked to grant the access permission.<br />Choose '**Allow Full Access**'.
   - In _Sync Enabled_, **switch the toggle button ON**, then configure:
   - Under _Sync as_, choose between 2 modes:
     - **All-Day Events**: Each habit syncs as a single all-day event, appearing at the top of your daily schedule regardless of when the habit is actually due, with the event's recurrence matching your habit's repeat settings.
     - **Based on Reminder Time**: Each of your habit's time-based reminders becomes its own individual event, respecting the specific times you've set. If a habit has multiple reminders in a day, you'll see multiple events for it. Each event defaults to 15 minutes long, which you can adjust later.
4. Under _Excluded Habits_, tap **\+ More**, and select the habits which stay out of the sync.
5. Once everything is set, tap **the く button** to save and return.

## How it works

When Habitify and Apple Calendar is connect successfully, **a new calendar "Habitify V2" will be created in your Apple Calendar** with all the habit events.

There's no syncing of check-in between Habitify and Calendar. Therefore, it may happen the case that your habit is completed but the Calendar still sends the reminders.

## **Removing synced events**

All habit events will be removed once you turn off the synchronization. 

1. In _Habitify app_, go to **Settings**.
2. Navigate to _Integration_, and tap to open **Apple Calendar**.
3. In _Sync Enabled_, **switch the toggle button OFF.**
4. Tap **the く button** to save and return.
