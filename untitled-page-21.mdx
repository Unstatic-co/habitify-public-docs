---
title: "Outlook Calendar"
---

Your habits don't exist separately from the rest of your day. They sit right alongside your meetings, appointments, and everything else on your calendar. 

This integration syncs your Habitify habits directly into Outlook Calendar, so you can see and manage them in the same place you already manage your schedule, instead of switching between two separate apps to get the full picture of your day.

## How it works

- For a habit without time-based reminders, it will be synced as an all-day event.
- For a habit with time-based reminders, each reminder will be synced as an event of the habit. Reminder time will be the start time.
- The description of your habit will be synced, too, as the description of the event.

**In your Outlook Calendar, a new calendar Habitify will be created for all the habit events synced from Habitify.** This can help you manage the events more easily when you can modify all the habit events by manage the Habitify calendar only.

## How to set it up

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    1. In _Habitify_ app, go to **Settings**
    2. Navigate to _Integration_, then tap to open **Outlook Calendar.**
    3. In _Outlook Calendar_'s setting screen, tap the **Connect** button.
    4. For the first time connecting, you may need to sign into your Outlook Calendar account and grant requested permission.
    5. Once the connection is set up successfully, you can configure:
       1. Under _On Event Delete,_ choose between the status update when you delete the habit's event synced to your Outlook Calendar, '**Mark as Failed**' or '**Mark as Skipped**'.
    6. Once everything is set, tap **the く button** in the top right corner to save and return.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. In _Habitify_ app, go to **Settings.**
    2. Navigate to _Integration_, then tap **Sync with Outlook Calendar**.
    3. In _Outlook Calendar_'s setting screen, tap the **Connect** button.
    4. For the first time connecting, you may need to sign into your Outlook Calendar account and grant requested permission.
    5. Once the connection is set up successfully, you can configure:
       1. Under _On Event Delete_, choose between the status update when you delete the habit's event synced to your Outlook Calendar, '**Mark as Failed**' or '**Mark as Skipped**'.
    6. Once everything is set, tap **the ← button** in the top right corner to save and return.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## **Limitations**

- If you delete the calendar Habitify from your Outlook Calendar, later change will not be synced. Please disconnect the integration and repeat the step to connect.
- Archiving or deleting your habit from Habitify will remove all the habit's event in your Outlook Calendar.
- Deleting all events or deleting all future events of the habit from Outlook Calendar will not mark your habit as skipped or failed.
