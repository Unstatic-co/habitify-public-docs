## Available plans

Habitify has 3 membership plans:

- **Free**: track up to 3 active habits at a time with 1 time reminder per habit. Check in daily, build your streaks, and keep basic notes on your progress.
- **Plus**: everything in Free, with unlimited habits and reminders, plus custom Areas to group your habits.
- **Pro**: everything in Plus, with more habit features and tools, advanced automation, and integrations with third-party apps.

## Payment options by platform

The billing options depend on where you subscribe.

| Platform | Free | Plus | Pro |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| iOS | ✓ | Annual | **Annual with 12-month commitment**, Annual, Lifetime |
| Android | ✓ | Annual | Monthly, Annual, Lifetime |
| Web | ✓ | Annual | Monthly, Annual, Lifetime |

- **Plus is Annual-only** on every platform. For a Monthly plan (Android and Web) or a one-time Lifetime purchase, choose Pro.
- **Lifetime is available only on Pro.** It's a single one-time payment, not a recurring subscription.

## Important: Understand the Annual plan with 12-month commitment on iOS

<Warning>
  On iOS, this is an **annual plan**, not a monthly one. You pay in 12 monthly installments, but you agree to complete all 12 payments. Cancelling does not stop the remaining payments.
</Warning>

This is Apple's payment option for annual subscriptions. It splits the cost of one full year of Pro into 12 smaller monthly payments instead of one upfront charge. If you prefer to pay once, choose the regular **Annual** plan.

### How it works

- The price of the Annual Pro plan is split into 12 installments.
- You pay the monthly installments and keep full Pro access throughout.
- After the 12th payment, the plan renews into a new 12-month commitment unless you've cancelled.

### What cancelling does

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling:

- **Keeps** your Pro access until the commitment ends.
- **Prevents** the subscription from renewing after the commitment ends.
- **Does not** stop the remaining monthly payments. They're still due.

To avoid renewing into another 12-month commitment, cancel at least one day before the renewal date.

<Note>
  Apple offers this option on iOS 26.4 or later, and not in the United States or Singapore. [Read more from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-vn/126901).
</Note>

### FAQs about Pro plan with 12-month commitment

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel and stop paying the commitment plan right away?">
    No. Cancelling an annual plan with a 12-month commitment doesn't end your payment commitment. You'll still be billed for the remaining months, and you'll keep Pro access until they're done. Cancelling only prevents the next renewal.

    If you want to pay for Pro without a commitment, you can subscribe on Android or the web, where the Pro Monthly plan doesn't carry a 12-month commitment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    1. On your iPhone or iPad, open the **Settings** app.
    2. Tap **your name**, then tap **Subscriptions**.
    3. Tap **Habitify**.
    4. Tap **Cancel Subscription**, then confirm.

    Habitify can't cancel an Apple subscription for you, because Apple manages billing for iOS purchases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I check how many payments I have left?">
    Open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Habitify**. Apple shows your completed and remaining payments there. Apple also sends an email, and a push notification if you've enabled them, before each renewal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cancelled. Why was I still charged?">
    If you cancelled before completing all 12 payments, the remaining monthly payments are still due. This is expected. The 12th payment is your last charge, as long as you cancelled before the renewal date.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund for the remaining payments?">
    Refunds for iOS purchases are handled by Apple, not Habitify. You can submit a request at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), and Apple decides whether to approve it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see the 12-month commitment option in the app. Why?">
    Apple doesn't offer this payment option in the United States or Singapore, and it requires iOS 26.4 or later. If either applies to you, choose Annual (one upfront payment) or Lifetime instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
