## Available Plans

There are 3 membership plans:

- **Free**: track up to 3 active habits at a time, 1 time reminder per habit; check in daily, build your streaks, and keep basic notes on your progress.
- **Plus**: track unlimited habits, and reminders, ability to add custom areas to group your habits.
- **Pro**: everything in plus, more habit features & tools, ultimate automation, and integrations with third party apps.

## Payment Options

Each paid plan supports different billing cycles to match your commitment:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Lifetime |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Habitify Plus | — | ✓ | — |
| Habitify Pro | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

A couple of things worth noting:

- **Plus is Annual-only.** If you want a monthly option, or a one-time Lifetime purchase, Pro is the plan to choose.
- **Lifetime is available only on Pro**, as a single one-time payment rather than a recurring subscription.
- **Pro's Monthly option on iOS app carries a 12-month commitment** rather than a cancel-anytime billing cycle. [Read more](https://support.apple.com/en-vn/126901).
