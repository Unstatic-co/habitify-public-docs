title: "Reminder"
---

Setting a reminder is one of the most effective ways to stay consistent with a new habit. A well-timed nudge often makes the difference between "I'll do it later" and actually doing it.

Habitify supports three types of reminders, each built for a different kind of cue:

- **Time-based reminders** : fires at a specific time you set.
- **Location-based reminders**: fires based on your location, when you arrive at or leave a place.
- **Habit-stacking reminders**: fires based on another habit's status, so one action naturally cues the next.

Here's how each one works, and how to set it up.

  <Tab title="iOS">
    ## Time-based reminders

    Time-based reminders let a notification fire at a specific time you set.

    To add a time-based reminder to your habit:

    1. In the detailed settings of your habit, tap to access **REMINDERS.**
    2. Under _Time_ section, tap **'\+ Add Time Reminder'.**
    3. Set the time using the wheel time picker
    4. Swith the **'Custom Schedule'** toggle to ON, if you want to assign the reminder to the specific day(s) in the week. <br />Then choose the day(s).
    5. Swith the toggle to enable one or all **the working modes** of the reminder on your device:
       - **_Show Live Activity_**: reminders will appear as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
       - **_Show as Alarm_**: reminder appears in full alarm-style alert, which you can dismiss or snooze like your waking-up alarms in every morning.
       - **Having both mode OFF**, your reminder still appears as a general push notification.
    6. Tap **the ✓ button** to save the reminder.
    7. Tap back to return to the habit's detailed settings

    ## Location-based reminders

    Instead of a fixed time, this reminder fires based on where you are, using the formula:

    > _When I am leaving/entering \[chosen location\], I will do \[habit\]. Remind me of \[habit\] when it's \[distance\] from my location to \[chosen location\]._

    To set it up:

    1. In the detailed settings of your habit, tap to access **REMINDERS**.
    2. Under _Locations_ section, tap '**\+ Add Location Reminder**'
    3. Tap on '**🔍︎ Search**' to find and select the address you want to base the reminder on.<br />Or, choose '**Current Location**' for quick.
    4. Choose the event type: **Entering** or **Leaving**.<br />In the map, hold and drag to adjust the distance from that address at which the reminder should trigger.
    5. Tap **Done** to save the reminder.
    6. Tap back to save and return to the habit's detailed settings.

    ## Habit-stacking reminders

    Habit Stacking is a behavioral technique that pairs a new habit with one you already do consistently. Instead of relying on a specific time to remember something, you use an action you're already completing as the trigger for the next one.

    The formula:

    > _Remind me of \[targeted habit\] when \[base habit\] is marked as \[Completed / Skipped / Failed\]._

    As soon as you log your base habit, Habitify immediately prompts the next one — creating a natural chain reaction through your routine. A few examples:

    - **Morning Workflow**: Remind me of _Read 10 Pages_ immediately when _Make Coffee_ is marked as _Completed_.
    - **Evening Routine**: Remind me of _Take a Walk_ after 10 minutes when _Have Dinner_ is marked as _Completed_.
    - **Flexible Backup Plan**: Remind me of _Run 5 km_ immediately when _Swim 1 Hour_ is marked as _Skipped_.

    To set it up:

    1. In the habit's detailed settings screen, tap **REMINDERS**.
    2. In the **Habit Stacking** section, tap '**\+ Add Habit Stacking'**.
    3. Under _Remind me to \[your habit\] when_:
       1. **choose the base habit**, the one you check in right before this one
       2. **choose the status of the base habit** that should trigger the reminder.
    4. Under _Send Notification_, choose:
       1. **Immediately** to fire the reminder right away,
       2. **After** then choose **\[amount of time\]** to add a short delay before it sends.
    5. Tap **Done** to save the new habit stacking reminders, and get back to the _Reminders_ screen.
    6. Tap back to return to the habit's settings screen.
