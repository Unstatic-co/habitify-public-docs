title: "Notes"
---

Note is a small, built-in diary attached to each habit , a place to write down your thoughts, feelings, and experiences as you track your progress.

Unlike a check-in, which only records _whether_ you did the habit, a Note lets you capture _why_ -  what was going through your mind that day, what got in the way, or what pushed you to keep going. You can also attach a photo to a note, so a workout, a meal, or a finished project can sit right alongside the day it happened.

Note isn't tied to one specific use, but it flexes with whatever you actually need that day. A few ways people use it:

- **Inspiration and vision:** Write down the reason you started the habit in the first place. On days when motivation runs low, having your original "why" saved right inside the habit can pull you back on track.
- **Diary and reflection:** Treat it as a running journal for the habit, like how you felt, how it went, what you noticed. Over time, this reflection makes it easier to spot what's actually helping you improve, instead of relying on memory alone.
- **Problem-solving:** On a day you couldn't complete the habit, jot down what got in the way. Writing the obstacle down, rather than letting it pass silently, makes it much easier to plan around it next time instead of hitting the same wall again.

This article will guide you around to use the Note.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    ## Adding new notes

    1. In _Journal_, swipe your habit left
    2. Tap on '**Add Note**'
    3. **Write your entry**, you can **attach a photo and/or a mood log** if you'd like.<br />By default, the note will be marked with the current date & time.
    4. Tap **the ✓ button** in the top right corner to save your new note.

    ## Reviewing all notes

    Currently in the iOS app, you can see back the notes you added for each habit:

    1. In _Journal_, tap on your habit.
    2. Swith to **'Notes'** section.

    From there you can edit or delete your notes.

    ## Editing existing notes

    To edit a note:

    1. In the view of all notes, tap on the note you want to edit.
    2. Edit your entry, add, change, or remove the photo and mood log if you'd like.
    3. Tap **the ✓ button** in the top right corner to save your changes.

    ## Removing existing notes

    To delete a note:

    1. In the view of all notes, press and hold on the note you want to remove.
    2. Choose **'Delete'**.

    ## FAQs

    <Accordion title="Where to view all notes across habits?">
      Currently, there is no common place for all notes on all habits. You can check the notes of each habit separately. In _Journal_, tap on the habit → then in the next screen, switch to tap '_Notes_'.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How to edit date & time of a note?">
      Currently in the iOS app, there is no available way to add notes for dates in past. The note is built to help you record the real-time experience for later real-time reflection.

      In case you still want to add notes for the past, please try to use Notes on website & desktop apps.
    </Accordion>

  <Tab title="Website & Desktop">
    ## Adding new notes

    In _Grid layout_:

    1. **Navigate the habit** you want to add a note for
    2. **Right-click on a cell of the date** you want to assign the note to
    3. choose **'Add Node'**
    4. Write your entry, add photos and a mood log if you'd like
    5. Click **'Save'**.

    In _List layout_:

    - **Option 1**: **Right-click** on the habit → Choose **'Add Note'** → Write the entry, add photos or a mood log if you'd like → Click **'Save'**.
    - **Option 2**: **Hover** on the habit → Click **the ••• button** at the right end → Choose **'Add Note'** → Write your entry, add photos or a mood log if you'd like → Click **'Save'**.

    <Note>
      **When you add a note within the List layout**, the note is assigned to current date & time by default, and it cannot be changed later.
    </Note>

    ## Reviewing existing notes

    In _Grid layout_, if on a specific date, you log a note for the habit, the related cell will be marked with a small triagle on the top right corner.

    Otherwise, there is a common place where you can see all the notes you've logged for a specific habit. To access the place:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the single progress view of a habit.">
        - In _Grid layout_, single-click on the habit name
        - In _List layout_, double-click on the habit name
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the sidebar of the single progress view.">
        - Navigate to the top right corner
        - Click **the sidebar toggle button** which is positioned in the right end.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click 'Notes' to see all notes added for the habit.">
        From this view, you can:

        - See all the existing notes
        - Add new notes
        - Edit the existing notes
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ## Editing existing notes

    To edit an existing note:

    - Open the section to view all notes within the habit's single progress view.
    - Navigate to the note you want to edit, and click the **••• button i**n the bottom right corner of the note.
    - Choose between:
      - '**Edit**': to edit the entry
      - '**Add image**': to attach photos
    - Optionally, click **the emoji icon** in the bottom left corner of the note to add or change the mood log.

    ## Removing existing notes

    To delete an existing note:

    - Open the section to view all notes within the habit's single progress view.
    - Navigate to the note you want to edit, and click **the ••• button** in the bottom right corner of the note.
    - Choose '**Delete**'.

    </Accordion>
