## Status of a Habit

Each day (or period), a good habit can land in one of three logged states:

- **Complete** — the goal was met for that period. Triggered automatically when logged progress reaches the goal value, or manually via the smart button / swipe right.
- **Fail** — you _planned_ to do the habit on that day but didn't. This counts as a miss and breaks your streak.
- **Skip** — the day wasn't meant to count at all (e.g., you were sick, on holiday, or otherwise pausing the habit temporarily). Skipped days are excluded from streak/completion calculations rather than counted as a miss.

Unmarked days simply carry no status until you log something or the day passes.

## Ways to record progress

<Tabs>
  <Tab title=" iOS">
    #### Smart Button

    A quick-action button sits to the left of each habit; its behavior adapts to the habit's goal type:

    - **"Done"** — for a goal of 1 time
    - **"✓ 1"** — for a goal of x times (x \> 1); tap repeatedly to increment
    - **"Timer"** — for duration-based goals; starts a countdown/stopwatch
    - **"Log"** — for other goal types (numeric, custom units)

    #### Swipe Right → Complete / Log Progress

    Swiping a habit right marks it **Complete**, or opens **Log Progress** to enter a specific value with two modes:

    - **Add** — adds the entered value to current progress (e.g., 2 km logged \+ 3 km added = 5 km)
    - **Set** — overwrites current progress with the entered value (e.g., 2 km logged, set to 3 km = 3 km)

    #### Swipe Left → Skip / Fail

    Swiping a habit left surfaces **Skip** or **Fail**. A confirmation prompt appears before the status is applied, with an optional note field for later reflection.
  </Tab>
