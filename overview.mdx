We provide a [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/) server that lets you connect your Habitify account to AI assistants. With MCP, you can manage your habits, log progress, and check your streaks using natural language through your favorite AI tool.

## **What You Can Do**

Once connected, you can ask your AI assistant to:

- List all your habits and their current status
- Mark habits as completed, skipped, or failed
- Log specific values for measurable habits
- View your streaks and progress

## **MCP Server URL**

```text
https://mcp.habitify.me/mcp
```

Use this URL when configuring your AI assistant. See the setup guides below for step-by-step instructions.

## **Authentication**

Our MCP server uses **OAuth 2.0 with dynamic client registration**. You don't need an API key — any MCP-compatible AI client can register automatically. When you connect for the first time, you'll be prompted to sign in to your Habitify account and authorize access.
