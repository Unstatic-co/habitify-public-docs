Habit Gate blocks the apps and websites you choose until you complete a habit. Websites are blocked in Chrome, Firefox, and your phone’s browser. To unlock them, you verify the habit with a photo, a location, an app’s screen time, health data, or an NFC tag. Each gate belongs to one habit.

## Quick Setup

To set up the habit gate for your habit:

- In the screen of _New Habit_ or _Edit Habit_, tap **Habit Gate**,
- Turn it on by choose option “**Block apps & websites**”
- In "_Apps & Websites_", add the apps or/and the websites should be blocked.
- Choose the verification method to verify the habit's completion and unblock the apps/websites.
- In "_Bypass Policy_", choose how strictly the block and verification should be.
- In "_After Verification_", choose what the app should execute after the verification is successful.
- Once everything is done, tap **←** to save the set up and go back to the habit's settings screen.

## Verification Methods

### Verify by Location

**How to Set up**

![Habit Gate Location Android 1](/images/HabitGate_Location_Android-1.png "Habit Gate Location Android 1")

1. In _Habit Gate_, under "_Verification Method_", select **Verify by Location**.
2. Tap on '**Pick a location**'
3. Select '**Current Location**' or **search for the place** which should be qualified for the verification, such as your gym or library.
4. Set the distance. This is how close you must be for the habit to count.
5. Tap ← to save and go back.

**How to Verify**

1. Go to the pinned place, you need to be inside the distance you set.
2. Open a blocked app or website to reach the blocking screen. Then tap **Verify**.
3. If you are within the area qualified for the verification, you can tap **Verify** button in this screen. <br />Otherwise, it will disable the button, and show how far it is from your location to the required area.

### Verify by Photo

**How to Set up**

![Habit Gate Photo Android 1](/images/HabitGate_Photo_Android-1.png "Habit Gate Photo Android 1")

1. In _Habit Gate_, under "_Verification Method_", select **Verify by Photo**.
2. Describe what the photo should show. Be specific. “_My running shoes on the doorstep_” works better than “_proof I ran_.”
3. Optionally, turn on the rules:
   1. Take a new photo
   2. Must be different from previous photos
4. Tap ← to save and go back.

**How to Verify**

1. Open a blocked app or website to reach the blocking screen. Then tap **Verify**.
3. Take a photo that matches your description.
4. Wait for Habitify’s AI to check the photo against your description:
   1. If your photo is not qualified, a reason will be provided. You can tap **Take another photo** and try again.
   2. If your photo is qualified, you will see a green ✔️, and you can tap '**Continue with [the blocked app/web]**' to use the app/web.

![Verify By Photo Android](/images/Verify-by-Photo_Android.png "Verify By Photo Android")

### Verify by Screen Time

**How to Set up**

![Habit Gate Screen Time Android 1](/images/HabitGate_ScreenTime_Android-1.png "Habit Gate Screen Time Android 1")

1. In _Habit Gate_, under "_Verification Method_", select **Verify by Screen Time**.
2. Choose the app you’ll use, such as Duolingo.
3. Set the target time, such as 30 minutes.
4. Tap ← to save and go back.

**How to Verify**

1. Open the app you chose and use it until you reach the target time.
2. Open a blocked app or website to reach the blocking screen. Then tap **Verify**.
3. If your screen time is qualified, you can contintue to tap **Verify** button. Otherwise, the button will be disabled.

![Verify By Screen Time 1](/images/Verify-by-Screen-Time-1.png "Verify By Screen Time 1")

### Verify by Health Connect

**How to Set up**

![Habit Gate Health Connect Android](/images/HabitGate_HealthConnect_Android.png "Habit Gate Health Connect Android")

1. In _Habit Gate_, under "_Verification Method_", select **Verify by Health Connect**.
2. In _Target activity_, Choose the activity you want to track, such as Steps or Calories Burned.
3. In _Goals_, set the required value to reach, such as 10,000 steps or 800 kcal.
4. Tap ← to save and go back.

**How to Verify**

1. Do the activity until you reach your goal. The data comes from your health app.
2. Open a blocked app or website to reach the blocking screen. Then tap **Verify**.
4. You can quickly check how much you do. For the first time, you may be requested to grant permission to access Health Connect.
5. If you reach the goal value, you can continue to tap the **Verify** button. Otherwise, the button will be disabled.

![Verify By Health Data](/images/Verify-by-Health-data.png "Verify By Health Data")

### Verify by NFC

**How to Set up**

![NFC Verify Set Up](/images/NFC-Verify-SetUp.png "NFC Verify Set Up")

1. In _Habit Gate_, under "_Verification Method_", select **Verify by NFC**.
2. Tap '**Scan a Tag to link...**'
3. Scan the NFC tag you want to link to this habit, leaving a short message which will appear in the blocking screen which might help you remember what to do to remove the blocking.
4. Tap ← to save and go back.

**How to Verify**

1. Open a blocked app or website to reach the blocking screen.
2. Tap **Verify** and choose **NFC**.
3. Hold your phone against the linked tag.

![Verify By NFC](/images/Verify-by-NFC.png "Verify By NFC")

## Bypass policy

There are 3 options of level on the bypass policy:

- **Easy:** You can open blocked apps at any time after a quick confirmation. No verification needed.
- **Friction:** You can open blocked apps for a short time, after Habitify asks you to confirm. Then they’re blocked again.
- **Strict:** Blocked apps stay blocked until the habit is verified. There is no way to skip the verification.

![Bypass Policy 1](/images/Bypass-Policy-1.png "Bypass Policy 1")

## After Verification

This setting controls what else Habitify does at the moment your verification is accepted. It runs automatically, so you don’t need to do anything extra once the gate opens.

There are 2 options:

- **Mark the habit as completed:** When your verification is accepted, Habitify checks off the habit for you. 
- **Save the photo to habit notes:** When a photo is accepted for the verification, Habitify saves it in that habit’s Note.

## FAQs

<Accordion title="**Do my apps get blocked again the next day?**">
  Yes. The gate resets at midnight (0:00). Once you verify a habit, your blocked apps and websites stay unlocked for the rest of that day. At 0:00 they are blocked again until you verify again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**What if the same app is blocked by more than one habit?**">
  You need to verify all of those habits. Verifying one habit doesn’t unlock the app while another habit that blocks it is still unverified.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**If I turn on several verification methods, which one do I see first?**">
  Habitify shows them in a fixed order: Location, Photo, Screen Time, Health Connect, then NFC. You can’t reorder them, but you can switch between methods for the verification by choosing "Other ways to verify" on the blocking screen.
</Accordion>
