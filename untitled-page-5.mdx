## Intro to Single Progress view

The **Single Progress View** is the detailed report screen for one habit.

To open this view of a habit:

- **iOS & Android apps**: in Journal view, tap on the habit.
- **Website & Desktop apps**: 
  - in Grid view, single-click on the habit
  - in List view, double-click on the habit

While the Journal only shows today's status, this view breaks down how you're performing over time: streaks, historical logs, consistency, and trends.

## Sections of the Single Progress View

| Section | What it shows |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Current Streak** | The number of continuous periods you've completed the habit's goal. If the habit has an **End Condition**, a progress bar toward that ultimate goal also appears here. |
| **Overview Report** | A grid of index numbers — Success/Completion rate, Failed, Skipped, and Total — summarizing your performance for the currently-viewed month or year, each compared against the previous range. |
| **Calendar View** | A visual calendar where each day is marked with a symbol reflecting its logged status (achieved, partial, skipped, or failed). Switchable between month and year range. |
| **Streaks Board** | The history of your longest streaks with the habit, not just the current one. |
| **Consistency** | Available for daily good habits only. A more forgiving companion to streak — instead of an all-or-nothing count, it scores your consistency as a rolling average over a selected window, with a comparison line against your past performance. |
| **Trending Column Chart** | A column chart of your habit's performance over time, switchable between Day, Week, Month, and Year views, with a dashed line marking your goal so you can see at a glance how you're tracking against it. |

## Single Progress view by Habit Goal

The sections above appear for every habit, but how each is calculated and what it shows shifts depending on whether the goal is per day, week, month, or year.

    ### **Current Streak**

    Streak for the weekly habit is weekly-based. It means, as long as you complete your weekly goal, your streak is counted up.

    Under the number of current streak, each of your last 7 weeks are represented as a progress bar, letting you easily see your completion rate recently.

    ### **Progress Break-Down**

    The grid report provides the details of your progress, where you can see the specific numbers reported on your achievement and development.

    There are 4 index numbers:

    - **Success (for iOS)**: displays the number of days you achieved your habit goal.
      **Completion rate (for Android):** displays the completion rate you accomplished on your habit for the currrently-viewing month or year.
    - **Failed**: shows the number of days you marked as failed for the habit.
    - **Skipped**: indicates the number of days you marked as skipped for the habit.
    - **Total**: reflects the total amount or number of times you have completed the habit, with the unit aligned to your goal's unit (e.g., minutes).

    Under each significant number, you can compare your achievement from the previous range ( ex, this month compared to the last month, this year compared to the prior year).

    #### **How are Success calculated?**

    The number of success days increases when the goal is accomplished. Consequently,

    - If the goal per week is completed, success days will be 7 days for the whole week.
    - If the goal per week is incomplete, success days will be 0 days for the whole week.

    Furthermore, if there are days marked with Skip or Fail, the total success days will be minus those days.

    _For example, if you achieve the weekly goal, then the total number of success days is 7. However, if you mark 1 day as Skipped and 2 days as Failed, then the total number is 5_

    ### **Calendar view**

    The calendar visually represents your habit progress in the period. Each day is marked with different symbols based on the status you check on your habit on that day:

    - **A solid colored circle**: you make progress on your habit
    - **An right-headed arrow**: you mark your habit as skipped
    - **A black cross**: you mark your habit as failed.

    You can change the time range from a month to a year to view your progress throughout the year.

    ### **Streaks Board**

    For the weekly habit, the streak board includes the progress bar of all weeks since the starting one.

    Your completion rate of each week is represented with the solid darker blue.

    The progress bars of your weeks are ordered vertically, making the comparison among weeks more intuitively.

    ### **Trending with the Column Chart**

    The column chart gives you a powerful visual of your habit performance over time.

    At the bottom of the chart, you can switch between **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**. This lets you see your progress from a daily snapshot to a long-term trend.

    When you select a time period, you'll see:

    - **Your Performance Trend:** Each column shows your total achievement for that period (for example, each day of the week or each month of the year).
    - **Exact Details:** Tap and hold any column to see the specific number you achieved for that single period.
    - **Your Average:** The chart also calculates your average achievement per period since you started the habit.
    - **Goal Threshold:** In the week view, you can see a dashed line which represents the goal you set for the period. Combining the dashed lines and your columns, you can see how much you grow comparing with yourself and your goal.
